# Privacy Policy

Effective 10 November 2026

Wordhold is an offline puzzle app. This policy explains what happens to information when you use it. The short version: Wordhold does not collect, transmit or share any personal information.

## Information we collect

None. Wordhold has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting. The app does not request the internet permission and cannot send data off your device.

## Information stored on your device

To show your scores, levels, streaks and progress, Wordhold stores the following on your device only:

- A record of each game you play: which game, the date, your score, how long it took and whether you finished it.
- Any unfinished game, so that you can resume it.

This information never leaves your device through Wordhold. We cannot see it and have no copy of it.

## Device backup

If you have turned on Android backup for your device, Android may include Wordhold's stored data in the backup kept with your Google account, so that it can be restored on a new device. That backup is operated by Google under your Google account settings, not by us. You can turn it off in your device's system settings.

## Deleting your data

You can delete everything Wordhold has stored at any time by choosing Settings, then Reset progress, or by uninstalling the app.

## Children

Wordhold does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Third parties

Wordhold contains no third-party advertising, tracking or analytics software, and shares no information with third parties.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be included in an app update and the effective date above will change.
